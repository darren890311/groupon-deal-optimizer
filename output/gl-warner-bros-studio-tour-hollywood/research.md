# Competitive & Market Research

## Review themes
**Positive:**
- Knowledgeable and entertaining tour guides — highlighted in multiple snippets, with one reviewer noting the guide 'clearly knew his stuff and made the tour fun and engaging instead of feeling scripted'
- Access to real Hollywood backlots and iconic sets, described as exceeding expectations ('NOT what we expected') in a positive sense by at least one reviewer
- Overall tour value praised by some visitors, with at least one reviewer stating 'this tour is well worth your time and money'
- Indoor stage room portions singled out as a highlight, mentioned as a stronger part of the experience

**Negative:**
- High cost relative to perceived value — multiple reviewers question whether the experience justifies the price, with one calling it 'not worth your time or money' and another saying it is 'expensive for what it is'
- Rushed pacing and crowd management issues — reviewers describe 'moving like cattle,' a rushed start, and 'long lines' consuming most of the visit time
- Limited number of sets available to see, with one reviewer explicitly citing 'a very limited number of sets' as a disappointment
- Inconsistent tour guide quality — at least one reviewer reports 'pretty bad' guide and service, contrasting sharply with positive guide mentions elsewhere

**Notable quotes:**
> 'Our tour guide was friendly, knowledgeable, and genuinely entertaining. He clearly knew his stuff and made the tour fun and engaging instead of feeling scripted'
> 'Expensive for what it is, moving like cattle to see a very limited number of sets.'
> 'most of the time was spent waiting in long lines, and it felt...'
> 'The indoor portion was better, but still not worth your time or money'
