# Competitive & Market Research

## Review themes
**Positive:**
- Overall enjoyment and repeat visit intent — at least one reviewer explicitly states 'we had a great time at the 2025 L.A. County Fair and can't wait to come back'
- Specialty events and shows praised highly — the 2025 Christmas Mooneyes car show called 'amazing' with 'a ton of awesome cars' (mentioned twice in snippets, suggesting strong enthusiasm)
- Art exhibits highlighted as a notable attraction — at least one reviewer specifically called out the art exhibit as worth photographing and sharing
- Discounted ticket deals appreciated — Ralphs special tickets at $12 apiece and a 'sunset special deal' both mentioned favorably as making admission more accessible
- Diverse programming and broad appeal — fair marketed and perceived as offering rides, food, concerts, and games for a wide audience

**Negative:**
- Food prices consistently described as outrageous — one reviewer noted 'average of $11 for a single' item with visible frustration, calling costs out explicitly
- Parking costs flagged as a pain point — $15 parking fee called out alongside food pricing, and an entire review snippet is dedicated solely to discussing the parking situation
- Parking logistics appear problematic enough to warrant detailed community warnings — one reviewer felt compelled to share 'some thoughts on the parking situation' as a heads-up to others
- Value concerns around overall spend — the combination of admission, parking, and food pricing suggests customers feel nickel-and-dimed despite discounted entry tickets
